ColorPicker, on web and native: a saturation and brightness area, hue and alpha sliders, a preview, a validated colour text field (hex, `rgb()`, `rgba()`, `hsl()`, `hsla()`, `transparent`), and swatches and recent colours as buttons. Its value is a CSS colour string; `onChange` fires as the colour changes and `onChangeComplete` when a drag or edit ends.
